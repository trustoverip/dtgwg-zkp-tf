## Attribution and maintenance

This guide began as an editorial extraction of the explanatory chapters of the DTG ZKP specification at revision `315cf4466bfcfe3d70c88a6706d0a6ca633c60c9`, and was restructured as a navigation companion to the specification. The source map records the extraction and its source digests. Working material that is not navigation (review packs, the verification worklist and dated research notes) is kept in the [evidence repository](https://github.com/mitchuski/dtgwg-zkp-mage); the specification's appendix tracks the task force's open decisions.

The Cryptographic Background is adapted from Mitchell Travers's earlier explanatory Zero Knowledge Spellbook material in the agentprivacy body of work. On 8 September 2026 Mitchell Travers authorized inclusion of the adapted text under CC BY 4.0. This relocation preserves attribution to that adapted text; it does not relicense the wider source work. Subsequent edits are identified by version control.

Guide documents are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Build scripts remain Apache-2.0 under the repository licence. The guide is informative and does not override the specification. The specification repository owns the records; the task-force repository owns this guide; implementers own their implementation sources; the evidence repository holds experiments and reproductions.
