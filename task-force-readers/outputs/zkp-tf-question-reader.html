Opening frame · about one minute

We are building an implementation-oriented ZKP specification for decentralized trust graphs: membership, relationships, selective disclosure, identifier control, status and delegated authority. Personhood and liveness are important use cases, rather than prerequisites for every graph participant. We would value your help identifying which parts of the paper’s framework generalize, which assumptions must stay attached, and what additional work our credential and task interfaces require.

Our immediate example is the community-anchored relationship proof with an offline voucher. We have construction records and some runnable components; we are not presenting the complete composition as implemented or independently verified. The intended output today is a precise first statement, its missing artifacts, a compatible construction path and a reviewable plan for the book.
